## 2. What the App does

The App connects to your HubSpot portal via HubSpot's OAuth authorization flow and performs an automated, read-only audit of your CRM data, deal pipelines, workflows, and user activity. It generates a scored health report with an AI-written summary and recommendations, viewable inside HubSpot.

The App does not create, edit, or delete any data in your HubSpot portal. It only reads data covered by the permissions you approve at installation.

## 3. Eligibility

## 4. Audit cadence

The App runs a new audit approximately once every 90 days per portal. This limit exists to keep analysis meaningful, since CRM data trends change gradually. You can request an audit at any time; if you are outside the eligible window, the App will indicate when your next audit is available.
